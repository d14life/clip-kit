# SixStars story options (2026-11-19)

Built from these files only: `captions.txt` in the 5 `GTA 6*` folders, and the autoclipper `*.json` transcripts (232 clips, after removing `non_gta.json` and `clips_crowd\xqc\1970-01-01`). Every event below comes from a caption or a transcript. **No clip was watched.** "Seconds" is the total clip length, not measured action time. Run a frame check before cutting.

- `media\vod\INDEX.md` and `media\longplay\INDEX.md` **do not exist yet**. `media\vod` holds only `xqc_mandy_6h32m27s-6h41m37s.mp4` (9 min) and `_work\kai_mandy` (in progress).
- File names are autoclipper bases. Each has `<base>_vertical.mp4` / `_horizontal.mp4` in `autoclipper\clips_ready\<streamer>\<date>\` or `clips_crowd\...`. All 79 bases were checked on disk.
- Score = virality-predictor rubric, 0-100, for TikTok/Shorts (hook 25, value 20, emotion 20, platform fit 15, share 10, trend 10). One moment hit by 3+ streamers and chaos/fails score up. Static talk scores down.
- Structure follows `autoclipper\VIRAL_LESSONS.md`: the hook asks a question, the build-up strengthens it, the payoff answers it, and a new question opens before the last one closes.
- Flags: **[SW]** swearing (almost every clip; bleep it). **[NUD]** nudity or sexual content (monetisation risk). **[RU]** Russian audio (needs English subtitles). **[SLUR]** slur.
- GTA text screens: "seen" means a caption says the event happened in the clip. "Overlay" means we add the screen; it is not confirmed in the footage.
