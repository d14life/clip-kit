## What we learned (short)

- **Guides and tips do best** for us: money glitch, loot spots, "you can't buy cars". Story clips do OK.
- **Hot topics:** Jason eaten by an alligator (16M views on X), GTA 5 vs 6 comparisons (5.6M; part of the viral one was AI, so use real footage only), Anti Furry Association (4.8M), trunk-looting jump scares (4.5M), car stolen with a cat in the back (4.4M), Paul Walker's Eclipse as a secret car (2.5M), the early-game money glitch (1.6M).
- **Every narrated short:** spoiler alert first if it shows plot, then the hook, then a "stay to the end" open loop on line 2 with a late payoff, then a clear closing question.
- **Clips:** EN only if score >= 8 and from the last 2 h of the streamer's latest stream; RU any age, score >= 5. No memes on clips. Blur nudity only.
