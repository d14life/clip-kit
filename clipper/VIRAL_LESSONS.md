## 2. Merged rules (weighted by views: the structure/format reels hold the most views)
**Structure (strongest and most repeated)**
- Hook, build-up, payoff. "Hook present question to viewer." The payoff must answer that question (572K, 938.8K, 1.5M).
- Keep an open loop to the end. Chain several hook/payoff pairs: "never a moment where you're not asking a question" (346.8K).
- Watch time is the thing: "Watchtime is the main factor" (1.5M). The 4.5M reel got average watch time above its own length.

**Hook**
- Visual hook plus text hook together (196K). A stitch hook (someone else's viral clip first) works because it makes the viewer ask why (1.3M, 114K).
- "Familiar but unexpected" (604K).
- Give the viewer a beat to form the question before the payoff (1.3M: "a little walk-in ... to give viewers time to process").

**Pacing and length**
- Quote: his hit reels include a 2-second reel and a long single-shot setup. [INF] His rule is about question-density, not a set length.
- Plain edit, one clear goal people wait for, build-up audio (114K).

**Captions and text**
- Caption as a character's view (213K). A text overlay that pops up to bait rewatching (4.5M).
- [INF] His own captions are minimal; no word-by-word style is mentioned anywhere.

**Sound**
- "Build-up audio to keep people watching" (114K). [INF] No other sound rules.

**Retention tricks**
- Rewatch bait: a pause challenge made people retry until they got it (4.5M).
- Pattern break to drag out comments and watch time (114K).
- Test several versions: "make five different versions, post all of them to trial reels and share the best performing one" (1.5M).

**Titles, descriptions and CTA**
- His captions are short, with 4-5 hashtags (#contentcreation #brandowner #advice #viral). A closing CTA is "follow for more ..." (nearly every reel).
- Add a restock/drop date or a "DM a screenshot" prompt at the end when selling (4.5M, 1.3M).

**Posting**
- Post consistent winners, post "a little less" and polish each (180K).
- Repost a winner after changing at least one component, one month later (180K, 1.5M).
- Baseline logic: three videos under baseline lower the next video's starting audience; three over raise it (119K, 1.5M).
- Posted about once a day in November per PicMany (inference from dates). He says he once posted 2 videos a day, 1500 videos in two years (1.2M reel).
- Every viral video has "at least one of the three E's": entertainment, educational, emotional value (1.5M).
- Take the format of an already viral video and plug in your own topic (2.4M, 1.5M, 180K).

**What kills reach (his words)**
- Weak hook, weak build-up or weak payoff (938.8K).
- A video with no open question: "once there are no more questions to ask there are no more reasons to watch" (346.8K).
- Three underperforming videos in a row (1.5M).
- Fully original ideas with no evidence they work (180K): "posting fully original videos without evidence it'll do well."
- Posting every day "hoping something works" (180K).
