# media/sfx licences

## Original (synthesised) - top level `*.wav`
Generated by `tools/scripts/make_sfx.py` from noise and sine maths. No third-party samples, so there is nothing to credit
or claim. Treat as ours, use freely. Files: whoosh_short/long/down, riser_1s/2s, impact_hit/heavy, sub_drop, bass_boom,
record_scratch, freeze_hit, pop_in, wasted_hit (original GTA-style stab + sub boom, NOT Rockstar audio),
bed_dark_70 (27.4 s loop), bed_tense_100 (19.2 s loop).

## Meme library - `memes/`
Copied unchanged from the original author's `autoclipper/fx/memes/` folder (myinstants.com / archive.org, downloaded earlier with
Damir's OK). These are NOT licence-clear: the GTA lines/jingles are Rockstar's, the others are creators' clips. Damir accepted
the Content ID claim risk (CLIPPING_GUIDE.md section 5). Do not put them in anything that must be claim-free; the
synthesised files above are the claim-free fallback (e.g. use `wasted_hit.wav` instead of the real Wasted sound).
